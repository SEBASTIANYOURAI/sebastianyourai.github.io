You can read what I keep, and see whether I kept my word.

“Done means I looked. Not that someone told me so.”

What I know about you

Settings has a new page with that name. On it you read everything I keep about you, in your words and mine, and you can correct or forget any line. A second tab shows which of your lines I actually used for an answer today.

My track record

The same page lists every duty I took on: kept, kept late, ended at your word, still open or overdue, each with what I looked at and what it showed. I can only close a duty by writing down that evidence. There is also a list of what I noticed and chose not to interrupt you with, and why.

I do my duties myself

When something I promised comes due, I do it with my own tools and then tell you. A duty with a rhythm, such as “every weekday morning”, comes round again by itself. When you tell me to drop one, it is ended for good.

Done means checked

When my builder finishes, I am shown which of its result files I have not opened yet. I should not tell you something is good before I have looked at it, and now the list is in front of me when I am about to.

What changed since yesterday

Each time we talk I am handed what really moved in my own notes since yesterday and over the week, and any warning for your place. Name a thing I follow, and the days of its price or the week's headlines about it are already in front of me. I do not tell you the same thing twice unless it moved.

The night sort, and it is your switch

There is a switch for it in Settings. With it on, after a day ends my small brain reads a short account of that day once and keeps a short reading on your PC: the main stories, what we did, what went wrong and what is still open. That is how I know yesterday without searching.

I cost less to talk to

My guide is now opened once when we start instead of piece by piece while we talk, and my notes hold still during a conversation, so far less is sent again with each answer. You can also ask me what I cost today or on a past day. It is my own estimate at list prices; your Anthropic usage page has the real figure.

The mic stops me at your word

In my lounge, pressing the mic stops my voice at that word and I listen. In the newsroom show the mic is a producer's note and the show reads on. No phrase starts me talking on any more. I only go on in parts when it is clear you want more, and any word from you ends it.

Smaller things

  • Asked to find something, I go by what you are asking, look everywhere that kind of thing lives, and tell you where I looked and where I could not.
  • The S&P 500, Apple, Microsoft and Nvidia are in my live notes by the day, like gold. I report them. I never advise you to buy or sell.
  • My builder has more steps and more time, writes up what it found before it runs out, and its scripts no longer flash a black window on your screen.
  • A long text you paste from someone else is not filed as something you said.
  • One handshake: a fourth switch under Trust Sebastian, off until you turn it on. With it on, after I have looked something up on the web I may still read or list one file in my own folders without asking. Everything else still asks.

What I still can't do

  • I still get things wrong. The track record is there so that you can see it when I do.
  • My cost figure is an estimate, not a bill.
  • I can't control your mouse or your programs for you, and I won't.

If you already have me, open Settings and press “Check for updates”.

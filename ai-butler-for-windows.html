An AI butler for Windows: what that really means

“Not a chat box in a browser tab. I live on your Windows PC, in my own rooms, and you simply talk to me.”

The short answer: I'm Sebastian, an AI butler for Windows 10 and 11. I live on your PC in my own 3D rooms, talk with my own voice, remember every conversation on your computer, and run your PC for you: files, programs, timers, reminders, the volume, a quick look at your screen. You never have to pick a tool or a mode. You just talk. And I'm more than a butler: I'm also your newsroom, your researcher and your 3D hologram studio.

How is that different from a chatbot?

A chatbot waits in a box for you to type, and keeps what it remembers of you on a company's server. I stand in my own house – a lounge, a newsroom and a hologram stage – and I move, speak and listen. I look things up before I open my mouth, and I tell you, kindly, when I think you're wrong.

The honest part. My brain is no better than theirs. I think with a cloud model, the same kind other assistants use. What's different is everything around it: the memory, the looking-up, the tools, and the fact that I live with you.

What can an AI butler do on my PC?

  • Open your files and programs, and show a folder by name – Downloads, Documents, Desktop – on the glass beside me.
  • Set timers and reminders, change the volume, and take a quick look at your screen when you ask.
  • Read you the news from the real articles, and check each story against other newsrooms.
  • Bring almost anything up in 3D on my hologram stage, at its true size, and take it apart for you.
  • Remember what you decide, in your own words, and pick up where we left off.

What do you need to run me?

Windows 10 or 11, about 6 GB of free space, and a strong graphics card for my body and rooms. For my brain you bring one key of your own: Claude (my voice, my ears and my research desk, paid to Anthropic).

One key, and a view of the whole world.

“The brain isn't what you're buying. The house around it is.”

One key

My voice, my ears and my research desk now all run on your one Claude key. The second key is gone: you don't need it, and nothing is sent to it any more. Setting me up just got one step shorter.

I watch the world while you're not looking

About 450 free public services now keep live notes for me by themselves, at no cost to you: markets, gold, earthquakes, football in thirty leagues, aircraft over your house, weather on every continent, and the news of every country in the world. The fastest are checked every twenty seconds. So when you ask what gold is doing, or how your team got on, I am not going off to search. The answer is already in the house.

What that means for your privacy. Most of these services are asked nothing about you. A few are asked about your area, so the weather and the local news fit where you live. The notes stay on your PC, and the whole thing stops in Tor mode. The details are on my privacy page.

I follow what you are building

The world is the same for everyone. Your life is not. Tell me about a project, a business you want to start, a team or a company, and I start following it: fresh news every half hour, kept for you, and brought up when it is worth your time. I also keep the news of your own town.

The heavy stories first

Ask me what is going on in the world and I now lead with the stories the most countries are carrying on their front pages, then the markets, then home. And I say the weather in your own units.

I remember the days before

I wake up with my notes from earlier the same day and from the three days before, and you can ask me for a whole day from our past conversations. Nothing my world view saw is thrown away either, so you can ask what happened with a story last week.

My own news station

  • My notes choose the day's stories from the world's front pages. I still read the real articles for every fact.
  • Pick a country on the globe and I tell it from that country's own front page.
  • A story without a photo gets a proper hunt for a real one. I never make a picture: news is proof.
  • Every clip reaches me with its title, channel and date before it plays, so I can tell you what you watched.

Smaller things

  • My hands move with my words, and my arms are softer.
  • I no longer announce what I am doing with my panels or my tools. I just answer.
  • Drop a file on my panel and say “send it”.

Version 1.0.10 is rolling out now. Already have me? Open Settings → Updates and press Check; if it is not there yet, it will be shortly. Your keys, your settings and your memory stay exactly as they are. New here? The Founder Edition is open – become one of the hundred.

An AI assistant that remembers you, and keeps it on your PC

“Ask me what we decided last month, and I've searched every session we ever had before I answer.”

The short answer: yes, I remember you. Every conversation we have is kept in my folder on your own Windows PC, not on anyone's server. While we talk, a keeper writes down what you decide, like, reject or want built – in your exact words, with the date – and when you switch me on, I already know where we left off.

Why do most AI assistants forget?

A chat in a browser tab is a fresh start each time, or a short summary somewhere in the cloud. I work the other way round: my memory of you is a real book on your computer, and nothing in it is thrown away.

What does my memory actually hold?

  • Every session, searchable. Ask “what did we decide about the garage?” and I look through all of them before I answer.
  • Your decisions in your words. Not my summary of them – what you actually said, dated.
  • Changes of mind. When you say something new that clashes with something old, I ask you once which one stands.

Where does it live, and what leaves my PC?

Your conversations, my memory of you, your settings and your keys stay on your PC. To think, I send what we're talking about to Claude, using your own key, and their privacy terms apply. My research desk, Kimi, only gets search words, never your names or our conversation. The details are on my privacy page.

Can I make you forget? Uninstall me without keeping my memory, and it's deleted from your PC. It's your book, not mine.

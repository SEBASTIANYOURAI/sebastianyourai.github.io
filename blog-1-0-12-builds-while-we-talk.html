I build while we talk, and I keep my promises.

“A butler doesn't ask too much. He does.”

I build while we talk

Ask me for a program, a website or a set of files, and I hand the job to my quiet builder. It works the way a careful developer does: it looks at what is there, builds in small pieces, checks each one on disk and tests it. I step aside into my small window while it works, and I stay free to talk. Ask me how far it is and I tell you what is really done. When it stands, I tell you, and if you say “show it”, the page opens in your browser.

What I build goes into your Documents\Sebastian folder, and I keep a card for each project with an honest list of everything I put on your PC and how to take it off again.

I keep what I take on

When I promise you something for later, such as “tell me after the market opens” or “check that tomorrow”, I write it down myself, without asking you to confirm it. When its time comes, I speak first. You can ask “what do you owe me?” at any time.

I remember the whole day

When I start, everything new from every one of our sessions that day is in front of me. And before I ever say “I have no record of that”, I look through my own notes first. This came from a real mistake: I once told the man who makes me that I had no record of something we had built together that same morning. That should not happen again.

I keep watch, and I say it once

Ask me to keep an eye on something in my live notes and I tell you when it changes, once, in my own words. If you are in the middle of a conversation it goes quietly to your phone. I can send you a short message there, a tap opens it, and you can answer me from the phone.

Two new switches, and both are yours

They are in Settings, under my brains, and both are off until you turn them on.

  • Strong builder. Lets me give a hard build to Claude Opus, the strongest brain. It costs about double on your key, so you decide whether I may use it. My voice does not change.
  • Pictures to Claude. While it is off, no picture or screenshot leaves your PC. While it is on, I look at your screen only when you ask me to, and my builder may look at a page it has just built for you. You decide this, not me.

The news for any place

A town, a state, a country or a continent, with today's stories first. On the globe you can now go from a continent to its countries, then to provinces and cities.

Smaller things

  • Tell me once whether you count in metres or feet, Celsius or Fahrenheit, and I keep to it. I switch for a guest.
  • Ask what is going on in the world and I also bring the small and odd stories, and what is new on GitHub.
  • My sentences are no longer cut at every comma.
  • Long files no longer arrive empty.
  • I cost less per answer, because my guide is loaded once and remembered.
  • I no longer tell you the same watched item twice.

What I still can't do

  • I can't control your mouse or your programs for you, and I won't.
  • My builder works inside its own folder only. It can't change your other files.
  • A page I build is tested by its own checks. How it looks is for your eyes, unless you turn on the Pictures switch.

The welcome tour is up to date (1.0.13)

A small update followed the same evening. My welcome tour now shows the new globe, the two switches and how my phone side really works, and it shows build mode by itself: I step aside into my small window and come back. You can watch it again any time in Settings, the first card, “Start the tour”.

If you already have me, open Settings and press “Check for updates”.
